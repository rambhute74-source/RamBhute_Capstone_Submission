The Library (Left Side)

Before answering any tickets, the system reads all 29 official company guides and indexes them into 59 bite-sized cards. This guarantees the AI only speaks from verified corporate truth, never guessing.

The Translator & Brain (Right Side)

When a customer asks a question, the system cleans the text, translates non-technical customer phrases into official manual titles, and retrieves the exact paragraph needed to solve the problem.

The Safety Gate (Bottom)

The AI is never allowed to guess. If confidence is below 80% or if the issue involves security or billing, it immediately hands off the ticket with a full briefing to a human support engineer.
